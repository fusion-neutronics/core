## Cite the nuclear data you use { #cite-the-nuclear-data-you-use }

!!! info "Credit the evaluators"
    Every result a calculation here produces rests on an evaluated nuclear data
    library: years of measurement, nuclear modelling and expert judgement by the
    evaluators who made it. When you publish or report results, cite the
    libraries your calculation used, alongside the code. A citation is how
    that work is credited, and how the people who make it can show that it is
    used.

Each reference below is the one the library's developers ask for. Cite every
library a calculation read: its cross sections, and each transmutation
subsection taken from another library. TENDL ``reactions`` with ENDF/B-VIII.1
``decay``, for instance, means citing both. ``data_releases`` on a run's results
records which library, and which release of it, the data came from. Throughout
these pages a library's name links to its reference: hover over it to see the
citation.

Each evaluation inside a library also names its own evaluators, and often the
paper it is based on, in its header (MF=1, MT=451). Where a result rests on a
few nuclides, cite those evaluations as well.

### ENDF/B-VIII.1 { #cite-endf-b8-1 }

Keyword ``endf-b8.1``. Produced by the Cross Section Evaluation Working Group
(CSEWG) and released by the
[National Nuclear Data Center](https://www.nndc.bnl.gov/endf-library/B-VIII.1/summary/),
Brookhaven National Laboratory.

- G.P.A. Nobre, R. Capote, M.T. Pigni et al., "ENDF/B-VIII.1: Updated Nuclear
  Reaction Data Library for Science and Applications", *Nuclear Data Sheets*
  **210** (2026) 1-224,
  doi:[10.1016/j.nds.2026.04.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nds.2026.04.001).
- The library itself:
  doi:[10.11578/endf/2571019](https://doi.org/10.11578/endf/2571019).

### JEFF-4.0 { #cite-jeff-4-0 }

Keyword ``jeff-4.0``. Produced by the Joint Evaluated Fission and Fusion (JEFF)
project, coordinated by the
[OECD Nuclear Energy Agency Data Bank](https://databank.io.oecd-nea.org/data/jeff/40/).
The journal paper on JEFF-4.0 is in preparation; until it is published, the NEA
asks for the data sets to be cited by their DOIs. ``jeff-4.0`` is built from
these four:

- Joint Evaluated Fission and Fusion Project, "JEFF-4.0 Evaluated Data:
  neutron data", OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (2025),
  doi:[10.82555/e9ajn-a3p20](https://doi.org/10.82555/e9ajn-a3p20). The
  neutron cross sections, ``reactions`` and ``branching``.
- Joint Evaluated Fission and Fusion Project, "JEFF-4.0 Evaluated Data:
  radioactive decay data", OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (2025),
  doi:[10.82555/tw0c6-t1386](https://doi.org/10.82555/tw0c6-t1386). The
  ``decay`` subsection.
- Joint Evaluated Fission and Fusion Project, "JEFF-4.0 Evaluated Data:
  neutron induced fission products yields", OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (2025),
  doi:[10.82555/trtwt-k2828](https://doi.org/10.82555/trtwt-k2828). The
  ``fission_yields`` subsection.
- Joint Evaluated Fission and Fusion Project, "JEFF-4.0 Evaluated Data: thermal
  scattering law data", OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (2025),
  doi:[10.82555/scmva-nqh68](https://doi.org/10.82555/scmva-nqh68). The
  thermal scattering tables.

### JENDL-5 { #cite-jendl-5-0 }

Keyword ``jendl-5.0``. Produced by the
[Nuclear Data Center of the Japan Atomic Energy Agency](https://wwwndc.jaea.go.jp/jendl/j5/j5.html)
(JAEA), which requires this reference to be cited wherever JENDL-5 is used:

- O. Iwamoto, N. Iwamoto, S. Kunieda, F. Minato et al., "Japanese evaluated
  nuclear data library version 5: JENDL-5", *Journal of Nuclear Science and
  Technology* **60** (2023) 1-60,
  doi:[10.1080/00223131.2022.2141903](https://doi.org/10.1080/00223131.2022.2141903).

### TENDL-2025 and TENDL-2017 { #cite-tendl }

Keywords ``tendl-2025`` and ``tendl-2017``. TENDL, the TALYS-based evaluated
nuclear data library, was founded in 2008 by A.J. Koning and D. Rochman, who
make it with the TALYS code system; J.-Ch. Sublet does its processing and its
fusion and activation benchmarking. Its developers ask for these references
(see the reference pages of
[TENDL-2025](https://tendl.imperial.ac.uk/tendl_2025/reference.html) and
[TENDL-2017](https://tendl.imperial.ac.uk/tendl_2017/reference.html)):

- The main publication: A.J. Koning, D. Rochman, "Modern Nuclear Data
  Evaluation with the TALYS Code System", *Nuclear Data Sheets* **113** (2012)
  2841-2934,
  doi:[10.1016/j.nds.2012.11.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nds.2012.11.002).
- A.J. Koning, D. Rochman, J.-Ch. Sublet, N. Dzysiuk, M. Fleming, S. van der
  Marck, "TENDL: Complete Nuclear Data Library for Innovative Nuclear Science
  and Technology", *Nuclear Data Sheets* **155** (2019) 1-55,
  doi:[10.1016/j.nds.2019.01.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nds.2019.01.002).

Name the release, TENDL-2025 or TENDL-2017, beside them: the two differ.

### FENDL-3.2d { #cite-fendl-3-2d }

Keyword ``fendl-3.2d``. The Fusion Evaluated Nuclear Data Library, coordinated
by the [IAEA Nuclear Data Section](https://nds.iaea.org/fendl/).

- G. Schnabel, D.L. Aldama, T. Bohm, U. Fischer, S. Kunieda, A. Trkov et al.,
  "FENDL: A library for fusion research and applications", *Nuclear Data
  Sheets* **193** (2024) 1-78,
  doi:[10.1016/j.nds.2024.01.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nds.2024.01.001).

### IRDFF-II { #cite-irdff-ii }

Keyword ``irdff-ii``. The International Reactor Dosimetry and Fusion File,
coordinated by the [IAEA Nuclear Data Section](https://www-nds.iaea.org/IRDFF/).

- A. Trkov, P.J. Griffin, S.P. Simakov, L.R. Greenwood, K.I. Zolotarev,
  R. Capote et al., "IRDFF-II: A New Neutron Metrology Library", *Nuclear Data
  Sheets* **163** (2020) 1-108,
  doi:[10.1016/j.nds.2019.12.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nds.2019.12.001).
